Current Operational Challenges

  • Root Shock & Severe Micronutrient Lockout

    Traditional concentrated caustic additions create localized high-pH plumes in irrigation channels, locking out iron, phosphorus, and calcium, resulting in stunted crop yields.

  • Worker Splash & Inhalation Exposure in Enclosed Spaces

    Handling liquid caustic drums and manual mixing in humid greenhouse environments exposes farm crews to severe chemical burns and toxic aerosol risks.

  • Agricultural Leachate & Runoff Discharge Fines

    Environmental agencies enforce strict limits on irrigation runoff discharge; unbuffered pH spikes cause immediate notices of violation.

Engineered On-Site Advantages

  • Continuous Micro-Dispersed pH Stabilization

    Synthesizes gentle, ultra-consistent catalytic hydroxide directly into primary fertigation manifolds, ensuring perfect root zone uptake without shock.

  • Automated Leachate & Runoff Neutralization

    In-line monitoring neutralizes greenhouse effluent before discharge, ensuring 100% compliance with local watershed and municipal sewer rules.

  • Zero Bulk Liquid Storage on Agricultural Property

    Eliminate chemical offloading, secondary containment berms, and hazardous chemical delivery trucks across farm roads.
