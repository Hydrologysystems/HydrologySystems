Current Operational Challenges

  • Hazardous Chemical Transportation to Remote Locations

    Hauling liquid sodium hydroxide (NaOH) to remote onshore basins or offshore marine platforms incurs extreme freight premiums, weather delays, and road weight restrictions.

  • Personnel Safety & High-Pressure Fluid Exposure

    Unloading high-strength caustic drums and totes in harsh environments exposes rig crews to acute chemical burn and inhalation risks.

  • Erratic Alkalinity in Brine & Mud Loops

    Batch chemical dumping results in sharp pH swings, polymer breakdown, and delayed response during unexpected sour gas or calcium kicks.

Engineered On-Site Solutions

  • On-Demand Wellsite Alkaline Generation

    Generate pure hydroxide solution directly from safe, dry precursors and site water on demand, completely bypassing bulk liquid chemical delivery logistics.

  • Continuous Automated pH Dosing

    In-line sensors pace generation to maintain optimal drill mud viscosity, neutralize sour gas in real time, and precipitate calcium/magnesium in lithium brine extraction loops.

  • Ruggedized Modular Skid Packaging

    Engineered in heavy-duty skid frames suitable for desert, arctic, or offshore platform environments with automated PLC integration.
