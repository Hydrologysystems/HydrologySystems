On-Site Catalytic Hydroxide for Crude Desalting & Atmospheric Overhead pH Control

Modern petroleum refineries consume tens of thousands of gallons of concentrated bulk sodium hydroxide monthly for atmospheric crude desalting, amine unit tail gas sweetening, and acidic condensate neutralization. Delivered 50% caustic requires heated tracing tanks, costly secondary containment, and constant offloading hazards. Hydrology Systems’ on-site catalytic hydroxide skids synthesize high-purity alkaline streams continuously, eliminating dangerous caustic truck logistics while delivering micro-dosed pH control that prevents overhead condensing unit corrosion.

Current Operational Challenges

  • High-Volume Hazardous Bulk Caustic Storage

    Managing 20,000+ gallon liquid caustic storage farms creates heavy OSHA Process Safety Management (PSM) compliance overhead, diking maintenance, and tank offloading spill risks.

  • Crude Overhead Condenser Chloride Corrosion

    Acidic chlorides in crude overhead streams cause severe localized corrosion when conventional bulk caustic pumps under-dose or experience delivery delays.

  • Winter Line Freezing at 54°F (12°C)

    Commercial 50% liquid caustic crystallizes at 54°F, requiring continuous electrical or steam tracing and causing emergency shutdowns during deep freezes.

Engineered On-Site Solutions

  • Electrochemical On-Site Hydroxide Synthesis

    Generate high-purity alkaline solutions on-demand directly at the refinery unit battery limits using safe solid precursors and plant water.

  • Automated Overhead pH Micro-Trimming

    Closed-loop feedback control provides smooth, micro-measured neutralization that stabilizes crude overhead condensate without chemical overshoots.

  • Decommissioning of Bulk Liquid Caustic Tank Farms

    Completely eliminate tanker truck deliveries, hazardous offloading zones, and PSM compliance burdens.
