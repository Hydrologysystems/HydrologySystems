Current Operational Challenges

  • Severe Bulk Tank Storage & PSM Regulations

    Storing bulk liquid sodium hydroxide near cooling towers triggers OSHA PSM scrutiny, containment diking maintenance, and winter line freezing.

  • Condenser Tube Pitting & Mineral Scale Fouling

    Acidic cooling water rapidly corrodes condenser tubes, while localized chemical over-dosing causes calcium carbonate scaling that degrades thermal efficiency.

  • High Freshwater Makeup & Surcharge Costs

    Poor alkalinity control forces excessive blowdown water dumping, inflating municipal sewer surcharges and freshwater utility bills.

Engineered On-Site Solutions

  • Automated On-Site Hydroxide Delivery

  • Closed-Loop Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) Trim

    Continuously balance pH and alkalinity to optimize cycles of concentration while protecting heat exchanger metallurgy.

  • Zero Hazard Footprint
