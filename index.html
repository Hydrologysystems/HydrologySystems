TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do Hydrology Systems skids eliminate delivered bulk caustic soda and soda ash?

On-site automated generation produces concentrated alkaline hydroxide (pH up to 12.0) on demand using electrochemical and catalytic cells, eliminating delivered 25-50% liquid caustic tankers, hazardous unloading, and bulk tank storage.

How is on-demand alkaline solution applied in industrial wastewater pH neutralization?

Industrial effluents with variable acidic loads receive continuous automated pH trim before municipal sewer or surface discharge, preventing localized over-dosing spikes and maintaining environmental permit compliance.

How does on-site pH control protect cooling towers and industrial process loops?

By providing continuous, automated alkalinity trim directly into makeup or circulating lines, cooling towers stabilize cycles of concentration and avoid both mineral scaling and pipe corrosion without hazardous chemical deliveries.

Can these systems handle high-volume greenhouse, hydroponic, and cultivation runoff?

Yes. Our catalytic hydroxide technology stabilizes pH directly in primary manifolds and neutralizes runoff before discharge, eliminating erratic chemical swings without harsh liquid chemicals.
