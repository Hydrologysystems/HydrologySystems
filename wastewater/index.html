Current Operational Challenges

  • Municipal Surcharge Fines & Environmental Violations

    Acidic effluent surges rapidly breach sewer discharge permit windows (pH 6.0–9.0), incurring steep municipal surcharges or shutdown orders.

  • Dangerous Exothermic Reactions in Pits

    Slug dosing 50% liquid caustic into concentrated acid retention basins generates dangerous boiling, spattering, and corrosive fumes.

  • Volatile Chemical Tanker Freight Bills

    Heavy industrial effluent neutralizers spend hundreds of thousands of dollars annually on delivered liquid caustic contracts and demurrage.

Engineered On-Site Solutions

  • High-Volume Automated Hydroxide Synthesis

    Generate high-purity alkaline neutralizer on-demand at the discharge basin to handle massive industrial flow rates.

  • Closed-Loop Dynamic Surge Balancing

    Automated PID control rapidly ramps generation to neutralize heavy acid dumps without lagging or chemical overshoot.

  • Smooth, Non-Boiling Chemical Dissipation

    Micro-dispersed catalytic hydroxide eliminates violent exothermic boiling and localized heat generation in neutralization tanks.
