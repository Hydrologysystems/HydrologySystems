FINANCIAL & OPERATIONAL ANALYSIS

Stop Flushing Capital: Delivered Bulk Caustic vs. On-Site Generation

Industrial facilities spend hundreds of thousands of dollars annually on delivered bulk liquid sodium hydroxide (NaOH) freight, storage tank maintenance, PSM compliance, and personnel splash risks. On-site catalytic generation eliminates chemical trucks and slashes chemical OPEX by 60% to 75%.

The True Burdens of Delivered Bulk Caustic

  • • High Freight Premiums: Hazmat tanker transport accounts for 30%–50% of monthly chemical invoices.
  • • Safety Liabilities: High-pressure chemical hose offloading creates severe employee splash burn risks.
  • • Storage Tank Degradation: 10,000+ gallon bulk tanks require continuous secondary containment and heated tracing.
  • • Sewer Surcharge Penalties: Slugging bulk caustic results in erratic discharge spikes that breach municipal permits.

The Engineered On-Site Advantage

  • • 60%–75% Lower OPEX: Produce high-purity alkaline streams on-demand from low-cost, dry solid precursors.
  • • Zero Bulk Liquid Storage: Decommission hazardous liquid storage tanks and eliminate hazmat tanker unloading.
  • • Closed-Loop Precision: Automated PLC trim paces chemical generation in real time for 100% municipal compliance.
  • • Fast Capital Payback: Typical industrial facilities achieve full skid equipment ROI within 6 to 14 months.
