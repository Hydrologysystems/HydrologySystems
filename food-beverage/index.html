Current Operational Challenges

  • Hazardous Bulk Caustic in Sanitary Food Facilities

    Storing thousands of gallons of 50% delivered liquid caustic near sanitary production areas creates major EHS liabilities and contamination risks.

  • Municipal Wastewater Surcharges & Out-of-Spec Spikes

    High organic load and acidic wash cycles cause effluent swings that trigger severe municipal sewer penalties and permit violations.

  • Inconsistent CIP Sanitation & Chemical Waste

    Manual chemical mixing causes batch-to-batch variation, line residue, and excessive chemical consumption during rinse cycles.

Engineered On-Site Advantages

  • On-Demand CIP Alkaline Generation

    Generate consistent, high-purity alkaline streams (up to pH 12) for degreasing, clean-in-place sanitization, and equipment washing on demand.

  • Automated Effluent Neutralization Skids

    Continuous automated dosing into wastewater discharge flumes guarantees compliance with local municipal POTW pH limits.

  • Worker Safety & EHS Compliance

    Eliminate chemical offloading hoses, tank farm inspections, and employee exposure to concentrated chemical burns.
